# Junteng Liu

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. I received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship at SJTU.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
